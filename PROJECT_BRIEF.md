# Rukn Al Yaqeen Business Setup

Rukn Al Yaqeen Business Setup (ركن اليقين) is a UAE business setup consultancy planning a bilingual website at rysetup.com on Hostinger. Its primary goal is to generate qualified leads and convert them into paying customers. The site serves entrepreneurs, investors, startups, SMEs, international investors, and established companies seeking outsourced PRO services.

## Brand and asset review

The selected mark is an RY monogram. Use deep navy (#10243E), premium gold (#C5A46D), ivory (#F5F3EE), and white (#FFFFFF) for a premium, approachable corporate design with clean layouts and subtle motion.

Four raster references were reviewed visually in the chat. The source files were unavailable in the checkout, and the original attachments were left intact. The captions are presentation labels.

- The standalone light-background mark uses navy for the upper R and outer Y, with gold through the inner R and Y. The stacked light-background logo changes the R joins and lower-left stroke, so its symbol cannot be reproduced from the standalone file by cropping.
- The Concept 1 board uses a mostly gold R, a separate Y with different junctions, and serif lettering. The separate stacked light logo uses a heavier sans-serif wordmark.
- The navy square avatar uses a white inner R stroke and a mostly gold Y. The board’s dark avatar uses white on the Y. The dark versions therefore also vary in color placement.
- The examples show English lockups. The Arabic lockup and exact flat color values still require approval.

The owner approved the standalone navy-and-gold RY direction. The website uses a first vector reconstruction from the visible reference, with sans-serif English lettering. Review the new vector and localized lockups on staging before final brand sign-off. Store the original files separately from exports once available in the checkout.

## Homepage and customer journey

1. A compact header offers service navigation, an EN/AR switch, and a free consultation action.
2. The hero explains the UAE setup and PRO offering in approved language. The primary action requests a free consultation. The secondary action opens the setup quiz.
3. Service cards route visitors to Business Setup, PRO Services, or Visa and Residency. A short Mainland and Free Zone comparison routes informed visitors to the relevant detail page.
4. An owner-approved process section sets expectations for inquiry, consultation, and next steps. Add credentials or trust signals as evidence becomes available.
5. FAQs address common objections and link to resources. A final consultation section and a mobile-friendly WhatsApp action give visitors an easy way to contact the business once the number is confirmed.

A visitor who knows the service lands on its detail page and submits a form with the service preselected. An undecided visitor answers a short quiz about activity, preferred location or jurisdiction, and visa needs, receives a preliminary recommendation, and can carry the result into the inquiry. An established company can go directly to PRO services and ask about recurring support. After a confirmed submission, show a clear receipt and any approved follow-up expectation.

## Lead capture, SEO, and measurement

- Use one shared inquiry flow for consultation and service forms. Ask for name, one contact method, service interest, and optionally a short business inquiry. Capture locale, source page, and approved attribution fields. Keep quiz answers local until a visitor chooses to share them.
- Make WhatsApp available with a verified business number and a short localized prefilled message. Keep personal information out of the link. Keep the live action gated until the number is confirmed.
- Build accessible mobile-first navigation, forms, and quiz interactions. Set Arabic RTL per page and verify keyboard use and reduced-motion behavior.
- Provide localized metadata, canonical URLs, reciprocal hreflang tags, XML sitemap, robots controls, and crawlable service and resource links. Index approved content only.
- Prepare Google Analytics 4, Google Ads conversion, and Meta Pixel hooks with configurable IDs and approved consent behavior. Record a successful lead once following server confirmation. Track quiz completions and WhatsApp clicks separately from confirmed leads. Keep measurement payloads free of personal information.
- Define a validated lead payload and delivery interface for later CRM integration. Phase 1 uses an approved business inbox or lead receiver. The cost calculator is a future feature pending approved prices, assumptions, and update ownership.
